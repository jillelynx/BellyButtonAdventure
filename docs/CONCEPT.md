# Belly Button Adventure — One-Page Concept
*Milestone 1 · written 2026-10-21 · status: draft for approval*

## The central experience
**"My best friend and I can go anywhere together."** The player should remember the feeling of exploring real places they love (and some they've only dreamed of) side by side with a brave little bear, and filling in their own adventure map one place at a time.

## Premise
Liev draws a huge treasure map of every place he has been or wants to go. One night, Belly Button's belly button starts to glow, and the map comes alive. Liev and Belly Button jump into it and land in **Belly Button's World**, a soft, stitched-together land made of felt hills, button flowers and yarn rivers. Pinned around this world are pages of Liev's map. Each page is a real place: Moscow, the dacha in Kratovo, West Cork, Washington DC, Lewes, the Grand Canyon, the Arizona desert, Antelope Canyon, Reading, New York City, Old Quebec City and Miami.

In every place, the friends hunt for the **Doodle Doors** Liev drew on his map. Each door leads to a side-view running-and-jumping level. Finishing a level earns a **Treasure Stamp** for that page. When every page is stamped, the treasure appears.

One map page is different: it is old, yellowed and smudged. It is a **Time Door** to a village in France in 1944, during World War II, where the friends help the French Resistance against the Nazi occupation. (Tone to be confirmed. See the open questions in PROJECT_RECORD.md.)

## Characters and what they bring to play
The player picks one at the start and can swap at any Doodle Door or on the map. **Both characters can finish everything.** Their abilities offer different routes and different fun, never locked content.

| | Liev (age 11) | Belly Button (plush bear) |
|---|---|---|
| Who he is | Brave, curious, sporty explorer who drew the map | Liev's soft, loyal best friend whose belly button is magic |
| Special move 1 | **Soccer kick:** kicks a ball to hit switches, knock things down and bonk enemies | **Belly Bounce:** lands on soft belly for an extra-high bounce |
| Special move 2 | **Climb:** grabs ledges and climbs ropes and walls marked with chalk | **Fluff Float:** puffs up to drift slowly down, crossing gaps |
| Customize | Outfit, hairstyle, captain's hat | (Later: accessories, maybe a scarf or bowtie) |
| What he lets the player feel | Capable and adventurous | Cozy, safe and a bit silly |

The other friend always follows nearby in Exploration mode and cheers, points at nearby secrets and waves the player back if they wander too far.

## Player motivation
- **Minute to minute:** something interesting is always in view, like a landmark, a glowing stamp, a curious animal or a door peeking out.
- **Over the whole game:** fill in the whole map, collect every stamp, unlock the next page, and find the treasure.

## Core loops
**Exploration:** arrive on a map page → spot a landmark → go there → discover a secret (a collectible, a fun fact, a character) → find a Doodle Door → the door is saved on the map.
**Platformer:** enter a Doodle Door → run, jump, bounce or climb through a side-view level themed to that place → reach the Treasure Stamp → pop back out in Exploration, and the map page fills with color.

## Progression
- Pages unlock gradually from Belly Button's World. Home in Reading comes first; nearby pages open as stamps are collected.
- Each place adds something new, such as snow in Moscow, waves in Lewes, wind in the Grand Canyon, light beams in Antelope Canyon, the subway in NYC and the city walls in Quebec.
- The map fills with color, and every found door stays on it for fast travel.

## The ending
With all stamps collected, the map folds itself into a big glowing book. The "treasure" is a scrapbook of everything the two friends did together: photos-style pictures of the places the player visited, in the order they visited them. Then Liev wakes up in bed with Belly Button, and the real map on his wall now has little stamps on it.

## Story through play
| Story beat | What carries it |
|---|---|
| The map comes alive | Playable: Liev's bedroom. The player clicks the map, the belly button glows, and they jump in. |
| A new world | First steps in Belly Button's World. Felt ground squishes, button flowers bounce. |
| Every place matters | Each page has its real landmarks to visit, not just a backdrop. |
| Time travel | The Time Door looks old. Colors turn sepia as you step through, then warm up as you help the village. |
| Friendship | The friend follows, helps and reacts. The ending scrapbook shows *your* journey. |
