# Visual Direction
*Milestone 1 · 2026-10-21 · draft for approval at the Milestone 2 visual checkpoint*

## The look in one line
**A handmade toy diorama.** Everything looks soft, rounded and touchable, lit by warm storybook light. Real places are recognizable from their landmarks and colors, but drawn with the same gentle, rounded hand as Belly Button himself.

Qualities borrowed (not copied) from reference games: the color harmony and calm of *Journey*, the sense of soft scale in *A Short Hike*, and the cozy "you can go anywhere" feeling of *Tchia*.

## Shapes and silhouettes
- Rounded edges everywhere. Nothing sharp except things that are meant to feel sharp (cacti, the Moscow towers' tips).
- Every character is readable as a silhouette: Liev's fringe or styled hair, Belly Button's tall rounded body with little round ears.
- Landmarks are slightly oversized so they are easy to spot and steer toward.

## Scale
- Liev is the measuring stick (about 1.45 m tall).
- **Suggested:** in the map world, Belly Button stands about to Liev's chest (about 1 m), big enough to be a real partner. *(Needs your approval.)*
- Doors, ledges and steps are sized so both characters fit and can jump them.

## Lighting and materials
- One warm sun plus soft sky fill. Shadows are soft and tinted, never pure black.
- **Plush and felt** (Belly Button, his World): soft matte surfaces with gentle edge glow so fabric looks fuzzy, plus visible stitched seams.
- **Real places:** smooth matte surfaces with simple color shading; no shiny plastic look.
- Water is soft and see-through near the shore.

## Interface style
- Looks like Liev's own drawings: thick marker lines, crayon colors, paper texture.
- Text is big and bold. Body text at least 22 px on a laptop screen; strong contrast.
- The map is a hand-drawn paper map, and found doors appear as little doodled doors you can click to travel.

## Animation style
- Bouncy and soft. Belly Button squishes when he lands; Liev's hair and jacket bounce when he runs.
- Characters blink, look around when idle, and look at the friend or nearby objects.

## Sound and music style
- Gentle, playful music in the spirit of each place (for example, an accordion-like tune in Quebec, a fiddle-like tune in West Cork, a balalaika-like tune in Moscow), all original.
- Soft, satisfying effects: squishy plush steps, a "pop" for stamps, a paper rustle for the map.
- Sounds are made in the game's own code where possible, so there are no licensing problems. Any outside sounds will be free-to-use and listed in CREDITS.md.
- Separate volume sliders: music, effects, ambience.

## Honest limits
The game draws everything itself in your web browser, in real time. Characters will look like **stylized storybook figures**, not photographs. Liev will be recognizable by his hair, freckles, ears, grin and clothes, not by a photo-real face. Screenshots in Milestone 2 will show what the game can really draw.
